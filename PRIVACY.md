# Privacy

Short version: **Engagement Rate collects nothing and sends nothing.**

## What it reads

On Instagram, X and YouTube it reads the like, comment, share, save, view and follower counts that the site has already loaded to show you the page. It reads them in your browser, from the same data the site uses to draw the page.

## What it stores

In your browser only (Chrome's extension storage):

- your settings, which Chrome may sync between your own devices if you use Chrome sync;
- follower counts of accounts you've seen, kept up to two weeks, so badges can show before a profile loads again;
- if Instagram follower lookups are on, the times of recent lookups, so it can keep to its limit.

Remove the extension and all of it is deleted.

## What it sends

Nothing. There are no servers, analytics, ads or trackers.

The one exception is optional and **off by default**: with **Look up Instagram followers** turned on, the extension asks Instagram (and only Instagram) for the profile of a post's author, the same request Instagram makes when you hover over a name. It goes from your browser to Instagram with your normal Instagram session, just like Instagram's own requests, and the answer stays in your browser.

## Permissions

`storage`, to remember the things listed above. The extension runs only on `instagram.com`, `x.com`, `twitter.com` and `youtube.com`.
